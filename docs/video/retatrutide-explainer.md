## Verified source data (do not invent beyond this)

Pulled from `src/data/products.json`, `src/data/documents.json`, `src/data/structures/`.

- **Name / class:** Retatrutide — investigational **triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist**
  (family: "GIP/GLP-1/Glucagon Triple Agonist").
- **CAS:** 2381089-83-2 · **Molecular weight:** 4731.33 g/mol
- **Receptor potency:** GIP-R EC50 ~0.04 nM · GLP-1R ~0.02 nM · GCGR ~1.1 nM (balanced triple activation).
- **Mechanism:** incretin arms (GIP + GLP-1) drive insulin secretion + satiety; glucagon arm raises
  energy expenditure via UCP-1 thermogenesis in brown adipose tissue, and suppresses hepatic glucose output.
- **Headline result:** NEJM Phase 2 (2023, Jastreboff) — **−24.2% mean body weight at 12 mg over 48 weeks**
  vs −2.1% placebo. (Also: Lancet 2023 T2D −16.94% weight / −2.02% HbA1c at 12 mg.)
- **Form / QC:** lyophilized 5 mg/vial · ≥98% HPLC purity · storage −20 °C desiccated ·
  testing standard HPLC VSR-QC-001 · USP <62>.
- **QC record:** Certificate of Analysis batch **RTT-2026-007**, VS Research Labs QC Division,
  standard ICH Q2(R1) · USP <62>, status CONTROLLED.
- **3D asset:** cryo-EM structure **PDB 8YW3** (retatrutide · GLP-1R · Gs complex, Zhao et al. 2024, public domain).
- **Status / compliance:** Investigational — Phase 3 ongoing, **not FDA approved**. Labeled
  **research use only — not for human use.**

## Robot Host — Character Design Brief (locked reference)

A brushed-gunmetal humanoid research automaton in the analog-industrial 80s sci-fi tradition — weathered
ALIEN-era hardware, not sleek modern chrome: riveted panel seams, exposed hydraulic tendons at the joints,
a matte steel shell scuffed from real use. Its face is a recessed dark visor housing a single warm amber
CRT-glow optic (with faint scanlines) as its only expressive element, and a thin thread of VS Research Labs
**gold** runs as inlaid trim along the collar and forearms — the one deliberate tie back to the brand inside
the grittier film world. Palette: gunmetal and brushed steel base, amber phosphor accent, cold teal
secondary practicals, all under tungsten haze. Personality: measured, precise, dryly authoritative — a
credible lab representative that states verified findings without hype, projecting the calm gravity of a
machine that has read every certificate of analysis and trusts only the data.
